## 2. Grant of Virtual Exhibition License

### 2.1 Scope of Grant

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Producer grants to Koncave Cinema a limited, non-exclusive (or exclusive within an agreed premiere exhibition window), non-transferable, sublicensable license to:

1. Ingest, encode, encrypt, transcode, and package the master video, audio, and metadata into multi-bitrate Adaptive Bitrate (ABR) HLS streams protected by Digital Rights Management (DRM).
2. Schedule, market, and virtually exhibit the Film to ticket-holding viewers across the Territory during agreed Showtime Windows.
3. Utilize approved trailers, posters, stills, loglines, and producer names across the Platform and promotional channels to market the premiere.

### 2.2 Territory & Window

- **Territory**: Worldwide (or geofenced territories as mutually agreed on a title schedule).
- **Reservation of Rights**: All rights not expressly granted to Koncave Cinema remain the sole and exclusive property of the Producer. The Producer retains 100% copyright ownership of the underlying screenplay, characters, master recordings, sequels, and ancillary merchandising.

## 3. Marketplace Intermediary & Agency Status (Tax & Fiduciary Invariant)

### 3.1 Platform as Fiduciary Intermediary

The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that **Koncave Cinema operates as a technology intermediary and digital exhibition marketplace (Agent)** facilitating ticket sales on behalf of the Producer (Principal).

### 3.2 Ownership of Box Office Proceeds

- Gross ticket box office revenues collected from purchasers belong to the **Producer** and are held in fiduciary trust/escrow by Koncave Cinema prior to scheduled settlement disbursement.
- **Gross Turnover Limitation (Finance Act 2020)**: In compliance with Section 105 of the Companies Income Tax Act (CITA), the Producer's box office proceeds (85%) do **not** constitute gross turnover or revenue of Koncave Cinema.
- Koncave Cinema's gross turnover is strictly limited to its earned platform distribution commission (15%), VIP room infrastructure markups (20%), and concession logistics margins.

## 4. Ticket Pricing, Revenue Split & Settlement Mechanics

### 4.1 Base Ticket Price ($P$)

- The Producer holds the discretionary authority to determine the **Base Ticket Price ($P$)** for each exhibition title, within platform safety boundaries:
  $$\text{₦}2,500.00 \le P \le \text{₦}10,000.00$$
  _(Standard recommended industry benchmark: **₦5,000.00**)_.
- The Base Ticket Price applies uniformly to Individual Watch and Public Hall communal admissions.

### 4.2 Revenue Sharing Architecture

For each virtual ticket sold, proceeds are partitioned according to the **Double-Margin Settlement Model**:

1. **Producer Revenue Share**: The Producer receives **85.00%** of the net ticket box office (configurable by mutual agreement between 50.00% and 95.00%).
2. **Koncave Base Commission**: Koncave Cinema retains **15.00%** of the base box office as its digital distribution and platform service fee.
3. **VIP Private Suite Infrastructure Markup**:
   - For Tier 3 (5-Seat Private Suites) and Tier 4 (10-Seat Private Suites), Koncave Cinema charges a **20% infrastructure markup** on top of the base box office:
     $$\text{Subtotal} = (P \times \text{Capacity}) \times 1.20$$
   - This 20% room premium is **retained 100% by Koncave Cinema** to fund real-time LiveKit Cloud WebRTC SFU audio infrastructure and cloud server overhead.
   - The Producer receives 85% of the underlying base seat volume ($P \times \text{Capacity} \times 85\%$), ensuring the Producer's contracted earnings are completely protected.

### 4.3 Payment Processing Fees & Statutory Taxes

- **Paystack Payment Gateway Fees**: Processing fees (1.5% + ₦100, capped at ₦2,000 for local cards) are paid directly by the ticket purchaser at checkout as a pass-through fee, ensuring zero erosion of the box office pool.
- **Value Added Tax (VAT)**: While Koncave Cinema qualifies as a small business under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and the Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025 (annual turnover of ₦100,000,000 or less and fixed assets of ₦250,000,000 or less), no VAT is collected. Once Koncave Cinema is required to register, 7.5% VAT will be collected from ticket purchasers and remitted to the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) without affecting Producer net box office payouts.
- **State Entertainment Tax**: No state entertainment or consumption tax is charged on, or deducted from, digital virtual streams.

## 6. Statutory Regulatory Compliance & NFVCB Certification

### 6.1 Mandatory Censors Board Classification

Under the **National Film and Video Censors Board (NFVCB) Act No. 85 of 1993**, all commercial motion pictures exhibited in Nigeria must possess valid statutory classification.

- The Producer represents and warrants that the Film has been officially submitted to, classified by, and approved for exhibition by the NFVCB.
- The Producer must provide a valid **NFVCB Certificate Number** and digital copy of the statutory certificate prior to publication.
- **Hard Technical Gate**: The Koncave Cinema backend hard-blocks premiere scheduling and public catalog display for any title lacking verified NFVCB certification.

### 6.2 Content Warranties

The Producer represents, warrants, and covenants that:

1. The Film does not infringe upon any third-party copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret, or intellectual property rights.
2. The Film contains no defamatory, obscene, child-exploitative, or seditious material violating the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
3. All musical compositions, synchronization rights, performances, and literary materials embedded within the Film have been fully cleared and paid for.

## 7. Stream Security, DRM & Anti-Piracy Commitments

Koncave Cinema warrants that it employs studio-grade security infrastructure to safeguard the Producer's valuable intellectual property against unauthorized reproduction:

1. **Digital Rights Management (DRM)**: Video segments are encrypted and protected via **Google Widevine Modular** (Widevine L1/L3) and **Apple FairPlay Streaming** (FPS) hardware-protected decryptors.
2. **Ephemeral Playback Authorization**: Stream manifests and playback tokens operate with short-lived time-to-live windows (180-second TTL), preventing re-sharing or deep-linking.
3. **Forensic Dynamic Watermarking**: All video viewports dynamically render an invisible and visible canvas watermark embedding the viewer's unique account ID, ticket pass code, and session timestamp. In the event of unauthorized screengrabs or recording, the watermark provides undeniable forensic proof to trace and prosecute the offender.
4. **Enforcement & Take-Down**: Koncave Cinema agrees to cooperate fully with the Producer and law enforcement agencies to execute takedown notices and prosecute infringers under the **Copyright Act 2022** and the **Cybercrimes Act 2015**.

1. **Term**: This Agreement shall commence upon execution or digital acceptance and shall remain in effect for the duration of the scheduled premiere window, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.

3. **Post-Exhibition De-Ingest**: Upon conclusion of the agreed exhibition window, Koncave Cinema shall archive the master video stream keys, revoke public access, and delete master video caches from edge CDN nodes within forty-eight (48) hours.
